## 1. Park Overview

Acadia National Park protects the highest rocky headlands along the U.S. Atlantic coastline, an abundance of habitats, and a rich cultural heritage. It draws ~4 million visits a year, making it one of the top 10 most-visited national parks. On Mount Desert Island, visitors enjoy:

- **27 miles** of historic motor roads
- **158 miles** of hiking trails
- **45 miles** of car-free granite carriage roads (perfect for group bike rides)

**Location & Getting There:** From Boston, take I-95 north to Augusta, ME, then Route 3 east to Ellsworth and on to Mount Desert Island (alternate: I-95 to Bangor, I-395 to US-1A to Ellsworth, then Route 3). Park address: 25 Visitor Center Road, Hulls Cove Visitor Center, Bar Harbor, ME 04609. Park phone: (207) 288-3338 · acadia_information@nps.gov

**Weather note for planning:** Summer runs 45–90°F; fall runs 30–70°F with first frost typically mid-October and first snowfall in November (averaging 73 lbs of snow seasonally). Pack layers regardless of season.

## 2. Activities On Offer

Acadia has something for every energy level — great for a mixed team:

- **Hiking** — front-country hiking on 158 miles of trails (e.g., Beech Mountain, Cadillac Mountain trails)
- **Biking** — car-free 45-mile carriage road network; also biking Cadillac Summit Road (weather permitting)
- **Camping** — both primitive and group camping options
- **Boating / Paddling** — kayaking, canoing, and stand-up paddleboarding on the lakes and coast
- **Climbing** — rock climbing on Cadillac Mountain
- **Fishing** — freshwater, fly, and saltwater fishing
- **Wildlife Watching & Birdwatching** — deer, birds, and marine life
- **Stargazing / Astronomy** — Acadia is an official International Dark Sky Park
- **Swimming** — freshwater and saltwater options
- **Guided Tours** — bus/shuttle and boat tours
- **Horseback Riding & Horse Trekking**
- **Picnicking** — plus the Junior Ranger Program for any teammates with kids
- **Winter options (off-season):** cross-country skiing, snowshoeing, snowmobiling, ice skating

## 3. Current Alerts, Closures & Hazards (as of Nov 12, 2026)

Two active alerts right now — **none affect a standard day trip or group hike**, but they matter if your dates slip into late November/December:

### ⚠️ Alert 1: Cadillac Summit Road Closed (Nov 9–25 & Dec 1–18)
- **Type:** Park Closure (area inaccessible)
- **Last updated:** Nov 4, 2026
- **Details:** Cadillac Summit Road is closed to **all users** (including bicyclists and pedestrians) from **Nov 9–25 and Dec 1–18** while construction resumes for an accessible pedestrian path between the East and West parking lots on Cadillac Mountain's summit. **Hiking trails on Cadillac Mountain remain open.** Note: Park Loop Road will close to motor vehicles for the season **Dec 1**, except access to Jordan Pond via Jordan Pond Road (Seal Harbor) and Sand Beach via Schooner Head Road.
- **Planning impact:** Avoid scheduling Cadillac Summit sunrise/sunset dividends or vehicle access to the summit during those windows. The Island Explorer bus does not serve Cadillac, and reservations are $6/vehicle for timed entry where required.

### ⚠️ Alert 2: Beech Mountain Loop Trail — Eastern Section Closed for Repairs
- **Type:** Park Closure (area inaccessible)
- **Last updated:** Sept 30, 2026
- **Details:** Since Sept 21, the **eastern section** of the Beech Mountain Loop Trail is closed from a junction just beyond the parking lot to just below the summit fire tower. **Three alternate routes to the summit remain open:** the outer loop of the Beech Mountain Loop Trail, Beech South Ridge Trail, and Beech West Ridge Trail.
- **Planning impact:** Easy to work around — just use one of the three alternate routes if Beech Mountain is on the itinerary.

**Bottom line on alerts:** No park-wide closures or hazards in effect today. Both are localized and manageable. Re-check NPS alerts (https://www.nps.gov/acad/index.htm) closer to your dates.

## 5. Quick Planning Notes

- **Entrance fees (if renting cars):** Private vehicle pass is $35 (valid 7 days, covers all occupants); motorcycle $30; per-person (walk/bike in) $20; youth 15 and under free. Acadia Annual Pass is $70.
- **Timed entry:** A $6/vehicle timed-entry reservation is required for certain locations (e.g., Cadillac Mountain) during peak seasons — check current requirements before booking.
- **Non-commercial groups (16+ people by bus/van):** organized group entrance fee applies ($20/adult, 15 and under free).
- **Pets:** must be on a leash no longer than 6 feet at all times.
- **Camping:** check campground availability well in advance if we're outdoors nights.
- **Contacts:** (207) 288-3338 · acadia_information@nps.gov · https://www.nps.gov/acad/index.htm
