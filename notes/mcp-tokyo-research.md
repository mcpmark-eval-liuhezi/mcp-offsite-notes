## Shibuya work spots

Coffee shops and co-working spots suitable for a group of about six for an afternoon, with names, addresses, hours and ratings where available. Source URLs are given for each entry.

- **Freeman Cafe (Metro Plaza Shibuya)** — 1Chome-16-14 MetroP Plaza 2F, Shibuya 150-0002. Open 9AM–11PM daily. Free Wi-Fi (password on every table), power outlets at most seats, fast Wi-Fi, big tables. Source: https://girleatworld.net/work-friendly-cafes-tokyo/
- **Valley Park Stand (Miyashita Park North 4F)** — 6Chome-20-10 On the R (4th) floor of Miyashita Park North, Shibuya 150-0001. Open 8AM–10PM daily. Power outlets on most seats, Wi-Fi 120Mbps, no time limit, floor-to-ceiling windows and leather seats. Source: https://girleatworld.net/work-friendly-cafes-tokyo/
- **White Glass Coffee** — 23-18 Visionary Arts 1F, Sakuragaokacho, Shibuya 150-0031. Open daily 8AM–8PM. Second-floor green oasis, free Wi-Fi and power outlets; seating limited to a maximum of two hours. Source: https://cotoacademy.com/best-cafes-in-shibuya-for-studying-and-remote-work-with-wifi-power-sockets/
- **Forest Library (Mori no Toshokan-shitsu)** — open every day 9AM–10:45PM. 1 hour = 1,100 yen, whole day = 3,300 yen; free Wi-Fi, power outlet, unlimited drinks (tea to juice) and a small food menu. Source: https://gonetravellingproductions.com/2025/05/13/late-night-tokyo-laptop-friendly-cafes/
- **FabCafe Tokyo** — 1-22-7 Dogenzaka, Shibuya-ku. Verified WiFi 97 Mbps; cafe + coworking + digital fabrication (3D printer, laser cutter, booked in advance). Source: https://geronimo-ai.com/best-cafes/tokyo
- **Streamer Coffee Company** — 1Chome-20-28 Shibuya 150-0002. Open 8AM–8PM daily. Power plugs on high chair seats (~10). Source: https://girleatworld.net/work-friendly-cafes-tokyo/
- **Trunk Bar Lounge (Trunk Hotel Shibuya)** — 5Chome-31 Jingumae, Shibuya 150-0001 (lobby). Open 8AM–11PM daily. Wi-Fi 360Mbps, power at ~20 working seats and lounge seats. Source: https://girleatworld.net/work-friendly-cafes-tokyo/
- **Marked Cafe (Shibuya Cast 1F)** — 1Chome-23-21 Shibuya Cast, Shibuya 150-0002. Open 9AM–6:30PM daily. Building Wi-Fi ~140Mbps, plugs available. Source: https://girleatworld.net/work-friendly-cafes-tokyo/
- **Hoshino Coffee MAGNET by SHIBUYA109 Shop** — 2F MAGNET by SHIBUYA109, Jinnan 1Chome-23-10, Shibuya 150-0041. Open daily 11:00–22:00. Rating 4/5 (42 reviews); good Scramble Crossing view, souffle pancakes; expect wait times at peak. Source: brave local search (https://brave.com)
