## MCP in the news

Recent coverage of the Model Context Protocol (MCP), an open standard introduced by Anthropic in late 2024 for connecting AI applications to tools and data sources.

- **ICP Launches Claude Connector for In-Conversation App Building** — Phemex News (11 hours ago). DFINITY Foundation's ICP launch of a Claude connector built on MCP, letting users build cloud engines and customer portals inside Claude conversations. Source: https://phemex.com/news/article/internet-computer-launches-claude-connector-enabling-app-building-directly-in-ai-conversations-102016

- **What is MCP? The Model Context Protocol, explained** — DEV Community (3 days ago). Overview of MCP as an open standard; Anthropic introduced it in late 2024 and it is now supported by many AI apps and tools. Source: https://dev.to/bytepenny/what-is-mcp-the-model-context-protocol-explained-35ld

- **Model Context Protocol (MCP) Explained for Lawyers and Legal Teams** — Legaldesire (2 days ago). Guide drawing on Anthropic's November 2024 MCP announcement and its December 2025 donation to the Agentic AI Foundation, plus the official spec and security best practices at modelcontextprotocol.io. Source: https://legaldesire.com/model-context-protocol

- **Model Context Protocol: The JSON-RPC Spec Trying to Standardize How AI Apps Talk to Data** — Starlog (2 days ago). Examination of MCP as a TypeScript-first protocol spec covering capability negotiation, sampling inversion, and schema-as-specification. Source: https://starlog.is/articles/ai-agents/modelcontextprotocol-modelcontextprotocol

- **Secure Your AI Agents – Free Model Context Protocol (MCP) Security Course** — freeCodeCamp (3 days ago). Course on the attack surface AI agents introduce when given tools, databases, and APIs. Source: https://freecodecamp.org/news/secure-your-ai-agents-free-model-context-protocol-mcp-security-course

- **Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF): What It Is and Who's In** — Lilah Bullock (20 minutes ago). The AAIF is a neutral, open foundation under the Linux Foundation, announced 9 December 2025, holding shared standards for AI agents outside any single company; it launched with Anthropic's MCP, Block's goose, and OpenAI's AGENTS.md. Source: https://lilachbullock.com/agentic-ai-foundation

- **Google adds interactive MCP apps to Gemini Enterprise** — Security Brief Asia (3 days ago). Upgrade gives enterprise users a live workspace for visual tasks in Gemini Enterprise, with edits synced. Source: https://securitybrief.asia/story/google-adds-interactive-mcp-apps-to-gemini-enterprise

- **Model Context Protocol Market to Reach US$ 28.46 Billion by 2035** — OpenPR (5 days ago). Market sized at ~USD 1.28B in 2025, projected to reach USD 28.46B by 2035 at 37.22% CAGR. Source: https://openpr.com/news/4673742/model-context-protocol-market-to-reach-us-28-46-billion-by-2035

- **Vultr MCP (Model Context Protocol) Server Currently Available in Public Preview** — Vultr Blog (1 week ago). Public-preview MCP server for better oversight of the stack and open-standards cloud infrastructure. Source: https://blogs.vultr.com/vultr-mcp-server-public-preview

- **Why the Model Context Protocol (MCP) Is Changing How We Connect AI Frameworks** — DEV Community (15 hours ago). Decoupling tools from language runtimes via MCP for complex multi-agent setups. Source: https://dev.to/flisk/why-the-model-context-protocol-mcp-is-changing-how-we-connect-ai-frameworks-1e8b
