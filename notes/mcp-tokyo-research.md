## MCP in the news

- **MCP spec revision `2026-07-28` released** — the latest stable spec revision: stateless at the protocol layer (no `initialize` handshake, no `Mcp-Session-Id`), mandatory `server/discover` discovery, deprecations of Roots and Sampling with a minimum 12-month functional lifetime, and the official MCP Registry publishing `server.json` manifests for discovery. [Source](https://github.com/modelcontextprotocol/modelcontextprotocol/releases/tag/2026-07-28)
- **MCP donated to the Agentic AI Foundation (AAIF)** — Linux Foundation directed fund co-founded by Anthropic, Block and OpenAI; in December 2025 Anthropic donated MCP to AAIF. MCP is now one of severalAF projects alongside Agents.md (OpenAI), open Env, cleanlab OpenEnv, and OpenSandbox (Alibaba), bringing the founders to 10 projects total. [Sources](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/linux-foundation-announces-the-formation-of-the-agentic-ai-foundation) · [詹姆斯](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_Context_Protocol)
- **Agent Skills standard donated by Anthropic to AAIF** — on 2026-10-22 together with the 10-project late join; Swarm intelligence tooling, versioned releases, and security guidance flagging versioned releases, a formal proposal process, and Discord feedback are on the roadmap. Among the original 70+ platforms integrating Agent Skills: ChatGPT, Copilot, VS Code, and Gemini; installs reported at ~1M skills, ~280M installs. [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/news/donating-agent-skills-to-the-agentic-ai-foundation)
- **State move for the spec ecosystem** — a repo is already wiring up the official MCP Registry via MCP publishers, publishing reverse-DNS `server.json` manifests during the current spec era and one back with the preceding stable-era version; spec lifecycle policy gives deprecated features at least a 12-month minimum functional lifetime. [Source](https://github.com/MattGyverLee/FlexToolsMCP/issues/369)
