- **Model Context Protocol** — Wikipedia. MCP is an open standard and open-source framework introduced by Anthropic in November 2024; it integrates with Microsoft Semantic Kernel and Azure OpenAI and can be deployed to Cloudflare. In April 2026 the AAIF held the MCP Dev Summit North America in New York City drawing approximately 1,200 attendees; by mid-2026 more than 10,000 MCP servers had reportedly been deployed in production with SDK downloads over 97 million times per month. The U.S. National Security Agency stated in a May 2026 report that MCP's rapid proliferation has outpaced the development of its security model. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Model_Context_Protocol
