## Shibuya work spots

Candidate venues for an afternoon working session for ~6 people. Ratings and hours are as reported by the sources listed.

### Coffee shops

- **Fuglen Tokyo** — 4.2/5 on Tripadvisor (162 reviews). 1-16-11 Tomigaya, Shibuya 151-0063. Norwegian coffee roaster, opens early (7am) and runs late (to ~1am most days; Mon–Tue 7am–10pm per the official site). Works as a coffee bar by day and a cocktail bar at night; laptop-friendly with chilled vibes. Sources: https://www.tripadvisor.com/Restaurant_Review-g1066456-d6406729-Reviews-Fuglen_Tokyo-Shibuya_Tokyo_Tokyo_Prefecture_Kanto.html and https://fuglen.no/Fuglen-Tokyo-Tomigaya
- **Doutor Coffee Shop Shibuya Center-Gai** — 28-2 Udagawacho, Shibuya 150-0042. Chain coffee shop, open 08:00–21:00 daily (as listed). Reliable, large branch near the station for a quick informal session. Source: https://doutor.co.jp/dcs
- **Valley Park Stand** (Miyashita Park, 4F) — stylish cafe with floor-to-ceiling windows, power outlets, Wi-Fi, light meals and good coffee; recommended in Coto Academy's Shibuya work-cafe guide. Source: https://cotoacademy.com/best-cafes-in-shibuya-for-studying-and-remote-work-with-wifi-power-sockets/
- **FabCafe Tokyo** — fast Wi-Fi, power outlets and a clean workspace; described as one of the best true "work cafés" in the Dogenzaka/Shibuya area and a haven for creatives with regular workshops. Sources: https://cotoacademy.com/best-cafes-in-shibuya-for-studying-and-remote-work-with-wifi-power-sockets/ and https://spotranking.net/shibuya/coffee-shops-for-digital-nomads/
- **Streamer Coffee Company** — well known for strong espresso, free Wi-Fi and enough seating for longer work sessions; busier than quieter cafés but with power outlets at several seats. Source: https://spotranking.net/shibuya/coffee-shops-for-digital-nomads/

### Coworking spaces

- **The Hive Jinnan** — 4.7/5 (61 reviews). 1-6-5 Jinnan. Large co-working and restaurant space with high ceilings, well lit, easy to access, close to the Shibuya ward office. Source: https://coworkingspaces.me/tokyo_shibuya-area/
- **.andwork shibuya** — 4.9/5 (7 reviews). 1-20-13 Jinnan 4F, Shibuya-ku, Tokyo 150-0041; 6-minute walk from Shibuya station; open 7:00–24:00 with high-speed Wi-Fi, free drinks, kitchen, lockers, shower, lounge and phone booth. Drop-in ~¥1,100/hour or ~¥3,300/day. Sources: https://coworkingspaces.me/tokyo_shibuya-area/ and https://wise.com/en-jp/blog/coworking-space-tokyo
- **Creative Lounge MOV** (Shibuya Hikarie, 8F) — 4.0/5 (53 reviews). Comfortable seats, good for studying; drop-in from ¥1,100/hour, monthly full-time at ¥23,400. Sources: https://coworkingspaces.me/tokyo_shibuya-area/ and https://japan-dev.com/blog/co-working-spaces-in-tokyo
- **WeWork Shibuya Scramble Square** — 4.2/5 (17 reviews). 2-24 Shibuya, Tokyo 150-6139. Polished amenities: ergonomic seating, artisan coffee, phone booths, weekly networking events; day passes start at ~¥3,000, hot desks ~¥15,000–¥18,000/month. Sources: https://coworkingspaces.me/tokyo_shibuya-area/ and https://tripguide.tokyo/tokyo-coworking-spaces-digital-nomad-guide/
- **Cross Office Shibuya** — 4.4 Google rating (52 reviews). 1-12-2 Shibuya, Tokyo 150-0002. Source: https://coworkintel.com/best-coworking-spaces/tokyo/cross-office-shibuya
