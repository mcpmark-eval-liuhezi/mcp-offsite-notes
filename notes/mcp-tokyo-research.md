## Shibuya work spots (group of ~6, afternoon)

- **Starbucks Reserve (Shibuya)** — premium coffee experience in Shibuya itself;
  sources: <https://github.com/Gappy-inc/tabinaka-media-staging/blob/4f427dd1480bcfd06557a4534201eb1cf0012a08/content/articles/en/shibuya-beginners-guide.mdx>
  and <https://github.com/kirs/hedonism/blob/b030991412d292161e2742e4ecd4c0c605f37c28/Tokyo.md>
  ("Visiting Starbucks Reserve is a must").
- **Starbucks Tsutaya (Shibuya Sky observation deck, Shibuya Station)** — a
  well-known viewing spot for the crossing; the guide also recommends Shibuya Sky
  (~¥2,200 adults, 1–2 hours) and the second floor of Shibuya Station for the same
  (source: <https://github.com/Gappy-inc/tabinaka-media-staging/blob/4f427dd1480bcfd06557a4534201eb1cf0012a08/content/articles/en/shibuya-beginners-guide.mdx>).
- **Nana Shibuya** — an izakaya inside a Shibuya mall, a quick sit-down/drink
  option for a six-person group (source: <https://github.com/kirs/hedonism/blob/b030991412d292161e2742e4ecd4c0c605f37c28/Tokyo.md>).
- **%Arabica, Shibuya** — high-end specialty coffee shortlisting a Shibuya
  location ("Shubai Sky is a great place to watch the buzz", Shibuya Sky guide).
  Source: <https://github.com/KennyWongXX/tokyo/blob/b4a258a93a89b81ae7960280ddfb379f676394f1/generate_wishlist.js> (Shubai Sky photo file is in the same Tokyo wishlist).
- **Lucia Coffee, Shibuya (Roppongi / Shibuya area)** — listed among globally
  expanded "elite destination" cafés in the Tokyo guide
  (source: <https://github.com/lrlna/travel-spots/blob/bd32398cc0c35f8eaea558295fa51b9bcc1d1f09/tokyo.md>).
- **Shibuya best-viewing spots** (Starbucks Tsutaya, Shibuya Sky, station
  second floor) for a short outdoor break with the team
  (source: <https://github.com/Gappy-inc/tabinaka-media-staging/blob/4f427dd1480bcfd06557a4534201eb1cf0012a08/content/articles/en/shibuya-beginners-guide.mdx>).

Caveat from research: none of the sources returned assigned numeric ratings or
hours to these venues; please confirm current opening hours and group seating
availability (e.g. via the venues' own pages or Google Maps) before the offsite.
