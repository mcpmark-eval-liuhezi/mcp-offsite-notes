# The Night Watch (SK-C-5) — Fact Sheet

- **Title (as recorded, Dutch):** Het korporaalschap van kapitein Frans Banninck Cocq en luitenant Willem van Ruytenburch, bekend als de 'Nachtwacht'
- **English title (collection page):** The Night Watch Militia Company of District II under the Command of Captain Frans Banninck Cocq
- **Maker:** Rembrandt van Rijn (signed and dated 'Rembrandt f 1642')
- **Dating:** 1642
- **Materials and technique:** oil on canvas
- **Dimensions (as recorded):** height 379.5 cm x width 453.5 cm (weights listed: 337 kg and 170 kg)
- **Object type:** painting
- **Current location:** On display in the Night Watch Gallery (Rijksmuseum, Amsterdam)

## 3. 18th-century story (Wikipedia + Rijksmuseum)

- Painted 1642 for the Kloveniersdoelen (Arquebusiers' Hall) in Amsterdam; commissioned around 1639 by Frans Banninck Cocq and seventeen militia members; Rembrandt was paid 1600 guilders.
- In 1715 the painting was moved to Amsterdam's Town Hall (Palace on the Dam), where it was trimmed on all four sides to fit between two columns — a common practice before the 19th century. This resulted in the create two figures on the left, the top of the arch, the balustrade, and the edge of the step; the missing portions have never been found.
- The 17th-century copy by Gerrit Lundens (1622-1683), on loan from the National Gallery London, shows the original composition.
- The title 'Night Watch' is a misnomer: a dark varnish gave the impression of a night scene; the varnish was所有的 removed only in the 1940s.
- Notable extras: attacked with a bread knife on 14 September 1975 (restored after four years), sprayed with acid on 6 April 1990 (only varnish layer affected), removed from the Rijksmuseum in September 1939 and stored in caves near Maastricht during WWII, and Operation Night Watch (public restoration since July 2019).
- Note: Wikipedia lists dimensions as 363 cm x 437 cm (142.9 in x 172.0 ft), which differ from the Rijksmuseum's current recorded 379.5 cm x 453.5 cm.
